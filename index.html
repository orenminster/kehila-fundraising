Our school

Who we are

Kehila Heschel School is an independent, not-for-profit Jewish day school serving Hamilton’s Jewish community. We offer a full academic programme from Kindergarten through Grade 5, integrating English, Hebrew, and French instruction.

Named in honour of Rabbi Abraham Joshua Heschel, our school reflects his values: intellectual curiosity, ethical responsibility, and deep connection to Jewish heritage and the broader world.

Our bilingual and trilingual graduates leave with strong academic foundations, Jewish identity, and the confidence to thrive in any environment.

The Annual Art Exhibition is one of our signature community events — a celebration of student creativity and an opportunity to support the programmes that make this school exceptional.

This event

Why it matters

Independent school tuition does not cover the full cost of the education we provide. Fundraising events like this one help bridge the gap, supporting bursaries, arts programming, STEM initiatives, and extracurricular activities.

This year’s exhibition highlights the creativity and learning of Kehila Heschel students, with special contributions from students at Hamilton Hebrew Academy and Temple Anshe Sholom, as well as local Jewish artists.

Every donation — large or small — makes a direct difference. Kehila Heschel School is a registered Canadian charity and issues tax receipts for all donations.
